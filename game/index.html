How to play

Goal: stop 15 waves of the Ink Tide from reaching the Keep. Every foe that gets through costs lives (brutes 2, the Titan 5). Lose them all and the run ends.

Build: click a tower below (or press 1–4), then click a glowing stone plot. Towers fire on their own at the foe furthest along the road.

Upgrade & sell: click a tower to upgrade it (two levels) or sell it back for 70% of what you spent.

Money: kills pay gold. Clearing a wave pays a bonus — plus 4% interest on the gold you banked. Saving pays; so does spending at the right moment.

Foes: Motes are fast and weak · Husks are the rank and file · Wraiths are very fast · Brutes are slow and tough · Spitters split into two Motes when killed · the Gloom Titan marches on waves 5, 10 and 15.

Tips: Gale Fans slow the tide so your other towers hit more often. Splash towers love tight packs. Mortars reach far — put them in the middle rows.
